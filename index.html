01 · Research

Research that connects
understanding to action.

My work spans long-video reasoning, temporal localization, multimodal pipelines, and real-robot VLA evaluation. I’m increasingly interested in models that keep broad generalization while learning subtle, domain-specific actions.

Published2026

Conditional Multi-Event Temporal Grounding in Long-Form Video

Multimodal AI · Long-video understanding · Temporal grounding

CoMET studies how to locate every event in a long video that satisfies a natural-language query with temporal and spatial conditions. My contributions included adaptive query planning, end-to-end pipeline integration, evaluation infrastructure, and video-understanding experiments.

Vision-Language-Action Robotics

OpenPI π0.5 · LeRobot · ROS2 · Real-robot evaluation

Worked on deploying and evaluating a VLA policy on a real Piper robot arm. I focused on autonomous rollout collection, LeRobot-compatible recording, success/failure feedback, multi-episode workflows, and investigating how rollout data could support later adaptation.

Current direction2026

Generalizable Fine-Grained Video Understanding

Domain adaptation · Temporal actions · Efficient specialization

Exploring how a broadly capable video model can learn subtle, fine-grained distinctions in narrow domains without becoming expensive to train or losing its ability to generalize. Potential domains include robotics, surgical video, sports, egocentric video, and games.

02 · Publication

Selected publication.

01

arXiv · cs.CV · 2026

Conditional Multi-Event Temporal Grounding in Long-Form Video

Yuanhao Zou, Arthad Kulkarni, Lucas Toñanez, Lincoln Spencer, Guangyu Sun, Tianxingjian Ding, Andong Deng, Yi Li, Shuangjun Liu, Yuan Li, Dashan Gao, Ning Bi, Taotao Jing, Shuai Zhang, Chen Chen

Introduces CoMET-Bench and CoMET-Agent for conditional multi-event temporal grounding in long-form video. The benchmark contains 2,789 queries over 600 videos averaging 33.8 minutes across five domains.
